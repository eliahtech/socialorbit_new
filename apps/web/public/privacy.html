PRIVACY

Privacy & Data Handling

Prelaunch informational draft. This is not a finalized privacy policy. A qualified privacy/legal review and final business contact details are required before production launch.

What the service is designed to process

Account information, workspace membership, content drafts, connected social account identifiers, temporary media, publication results, billing records and support requests.

Social connections

Official OAuth authorization is used where supported. Provider credentials are encrypted on the backend and are not exposed in the browser.

Temporary media

Media uploaded or generated through SocialOrbit is subject to a seven-day storage deletion policy. Content metadata and billing records follow separate retention needs that must be finalized in the legal policy.

Your choices

Users can disconnect social accounts, edit their profiles, and contact support. Account deletion and data-export workflows are still being implemented.
