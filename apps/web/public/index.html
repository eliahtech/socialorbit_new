QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Does SocialOrbit support every action on every platform?

No. Publishing, discovery, messaging and analytics depend on platform APIs and account approvals. Unsupported actions are disabled rather than simulated.

What happens when I run out of AI credits?

AI actions requiring additional credits stop. Your other available subscription functionality remains accessible.

Can I upload videos?

No. SocialOrbit does not store or process video files. Videos that are already on YouTube or Facebook are shared as links. Uploaded images and generated media are scheduled for deletion after seven days; metadata and publishing history are separate.

Can I use SocialOrbit from my phone?

The responsive website is designed for phones, and native Android/iOS applications are planned using a shared backend.
